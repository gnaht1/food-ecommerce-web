Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

We are committed to delivering your fresh ingredients promptly. We offer the following shipping options:

  • Standard Shipping: 2-3 business days.
  • Express Shipping: Next business day delivery.
  • Scheduled Delivery: Choose a specific date and time for your delivery.

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and the selected shipping method. We currently ship to all locations within the city limits.

Returns Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you are not completely satisfied with the quality of our ingredients, please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order.

We offer the following solutions for returns:

  • Full Refund: If the ingredients are spoiled or not as described, we will issue a full refund to your original payment method.
  • Store Credit: You can opt for store credit to use on your next purchase.
  • Replacement: We can send a replacement for the unsatisfactory items with your next order.

To initiate a return, please contact our customer support team with your order number and a description of the issue. We may request a photo of the product to help us improve our services.
